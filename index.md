# Indian Astronomers Around the World

> A global platform for Indian astronomers (faculty, postdocs, PhD students, researchers) to exchange knowledge, foster collaboration, and host online colloquia, workshops, lectures, and events.

## Who this is for

- Indian astronomers and allied researchers working anywhere in the world
- Students and early-career researchers seeking mentorship and collaborations
- Organizers of seminars, workshops, and reading groups

## What we do

- Host regular online colloquia and workshops
- Maintain an events calendar and archive (recordings & slides)
- Provide a forum for collaborations and knowledge exchange

- [Browse upcoming and past events](/events/)
