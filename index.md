**Indian Astronomers Around the World (IAAW)** is a community-driven initiative connecting Indian astronomers working across the globe — including faculty members, postdoctoral researchers, PhD students, and early-career scientists.

The initiative aims to foster knowledge exchange, collaboration, and mentorship within the Indian astronomy and astrophysics community worldwide.

---

## What We Do

We organize and support a range of academic activities, including:

- **Online colloquia** by Indian astronomers working at institutions around the world  
- **Hands-on workshops** and lecture series on research methods, instrumentation, and data analysis  
- **Discussion forums** to exchange ideas, experiences, and best practices  
- **Community building** to encourage collaborations across institutions and countries  

All activities are designed to be inclusive, accessible, and academically rigorous.

- Faculty members
