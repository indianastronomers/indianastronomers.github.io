## Workshop Title

## Description

<p>
Indian Astronomers Around the World (IAAW) is organizing an **online hands-on workshop** on **TOPCAT**, a widely used astronomical data analysis tool for working with large catalogs and survey data.

<p>

## Abstract and Outline

<div style="text-align: justify;">

<p>
Modern astronomical observations produce large amounts of data, much
of it in the form of source catalogues and other tables.  TOPCAT is an
interactive desktop application, developed over the last two decades,
that helps astronomers to work with these products.  It facilitates
operations such as data access, visualisation, selection management,
calculations and crossmatching so that astronomers can concentrate on the
scientific meaning of the observations rather than the mechanics of moving
data around.  It has particular strengths in interactive exploration of
large and small datasets and in providing access to external data via
the Virtual Observatory.
</p>

<p>
In the first part of the presentation I will describe the main aims and
capabilities of the software.  In the second part I will run through some
live demonstrations of using it for science analysis of data from the
Gaia source catalogue.  Participants can either just watch the demos or
follow along as a hands-on exercise.  A detailed script describing how
to reproduce the demonstrations is provided for use either at the time
or for later reference.  There will be time for questions and discussion.
</p>

</div>
