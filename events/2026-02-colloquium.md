**Speaker:** Dr. Ananya Rao (Institute of Astronomy, Cambridge)  
**Affiliation:** Institute of Astronomy, University of Cambridge

**Title:** Probing Exoplanet Atmospheres with High-resolution Spectroscopy

**Abstract:**

We discuss recent advances in high-resolution spectroscopy for detecting molecular signatures in exoplanet atmospheres. The talk will cover instrumentation, data analysis pipelines, and case studies highlighting detection of water vapor and carbon-bearing species.
